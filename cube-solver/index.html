What do the letters mean? (move notation)

Hold the cube with the front face toward you — the same orientation you used when entering/scanning it (Up face on top).

  • U Up, D Down, L Left, R Right, F Front, B Back
  • A plain letter (e.g. R) = turn that face clockwise 90° (as if you were looking straight at that face).
  • An apostrophe (e.g. R') = counter-clockwise 90°.
  • A 2 (e.g. R2) = half turn, 180°.
